Artist Statement

I’m RA.S — I build bright systems that breathe.

My practice lives between code and canvas. I chase moments where precision meets impulse, rendering textures that feel both designed and discovered.

The palette is intentional: red and white for clarity and charge; yellow and blue for accent and lift— a marker’s memory across digital paper.
